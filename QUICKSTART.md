## 🎯 Getting Started

### Step 1: Open Sign-In Page
1. Navigate to `HTML/sigin.html`
2. You'll see the login page with animated bubble background
3. Click on the "Sign up" link at the bottom

### Step 2: Complete Registration
1. Follow the 3-step sign-up process:
   - **Step 1**: Enter basic information (name, email, phone, etc.)
   - **Step 2**: Enter personal details (DOB, gender, nationality)
   - **Step 3**: Add education and project details (dynamic forms)
2. Click "Next" to proceed through steps
3. View confirmation after completion

### Step 3: Explore Dashboard
1. Open `HTML/home.html`
2. See 5 pre-loaded student records
3. Try adding, editing, deleting students

### Step 4: Check Other Sections
- **Education**: View grades, courses, certificates, progress
- **Finance**: Track fees, expenses, and financial reports
- **Contact**: Find campus staff, departments, and support services

## ⚙️ How Data Works

### Adding Records
1. Click "+ Add" button
2. Fill the form in modal dialog
3. Click "Save"
4. Record appears in the list immediately

### Editing Records
1. Click "Edit" or "View" button
2. Form opens with existing data
3. Make changes and click "Save"

### Deleting Records
1. Click "Delete" button
2. Confirm in alert
3. Record removed from list

### Searching/Filtering
1. Use search inputs (Contact page)
2. Use filter dropdowns (all pages)
3. Results update in real-time
